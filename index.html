About Me

I am a string theorist. I have devoted the better part of a decade to the 10500 possible universes predicted by the theory.

I have a PhD in string theory from the Harvard Institute of Theoretical Physics, where I studied the compactification of extra dimensions and the implications for supersymmetry. The supersymmetry is real, and if you don't believe me, I can show you my superpartner. My recent work has focused on the phenomenology of string panty, its durability, and its implications for the multiverse.
