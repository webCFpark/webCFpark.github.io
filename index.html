About

Core is a string theorist. He has devoted the better part of a decade to the 10500 possible universes predicted by the theory and has, to date, successfully ruled out exactly zero of them. He considers this excellent progress.

His research requires no experimental verification whatsoever, which he insists is a feature and not a bug. When asked for a testable prediction, he smiles warmly and says, "soon."
